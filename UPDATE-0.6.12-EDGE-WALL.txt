Nikogram 0.6.12 - PREPARED ONLY, NOT LIVE VERIFIED

Edge is strictly per-selector. Real Edge cannot enable/override Fake Edge and
Fake Edge cannot enable/override Real Edge. Non-Edge yaws keep their existing
dispatch, base and offsets. Separation never forces the other selector to move.

Real Edge replaces averaged occlusion with nearest vertical head-height wall
detection and a wall-fit objective: missing head margins, head clearance, then
body clearance. Mouse/fake direction cannot override that cover ordering.
Fake Edge alone seeks exposure/separation relative to the configured real.
With both Edge, real is solved for cover before fake exposure. Without usable
cover, only Edge yaw outputs use the opposite-angle fallback.

Only AntiAim.cpp, EdgeCoverPolicy.h and the yaw tooltips in Vars.h change from
0.6.10. Screenshot, Dapper, menu rendering and packet/body controllers unchanged.
The rejected auto-pair behavior from the uninstalled 0.6.11 preparation is gone.

Analytic geometry and selector-isolation checks do not establish exact native
animated/server head protection. Native TF2 wall-following remains unconfirmed.
Prepared only; no live session interaction or request for another playtest.
